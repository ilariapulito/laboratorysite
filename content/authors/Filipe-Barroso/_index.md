Born in Guimarães, Portugal, in 1988. Filipe received his MSc degree in Biomedical Engineering in September 2011 and his PhD in Biomedical Engineering in March 2016 from the University of Minho, Portugal.

In addition to coordinating the EU Project **EXTEND**, he performed research work for the EU Project **BETTER** (contract no. 247935) and Project **HYPER** (CONSOLIDER-INGENIO 2010) during his PhD research as a Research Fellow in the Neural Rehabilitation Group at CSIC.

From 2016 to February 2018, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Feinberg School of Medicine, Northwestern University (Chicago, IL, USA). There, he expanded his expertise in motor control, biomechanics, brain-machine interfaces (BMIs), and neuroplasticity after spinal cord injury (SCI), contributing to projects funded by the Craig H. Neilsen Foundation (*Development of an FES neuroprosthesis for rehabilitation following SCI*) and the NIH (*The Neural Control of Internal Joint State*, 5R01NS086973-04).
