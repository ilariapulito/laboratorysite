abstract: "Essential tremor (ET) is driven by abnormal neural oscillatory activity within the cerebello-thalamo-cortical network. In this study, we identify neurophysiological mechanisms underlying tremor suppression induced by customized, closed-loop sensory peripheral electrical stimulation (PES). By modulating thalamic and spinal circuits in real time based on user motor activity, this non-invasive approach offers a personalized alternative to reduce tremor severity and improve motor performance in daily activities."
