abstract: "Pathological tremor is a common movement disorder. Closed-loop control systems based on Pulsed Electrical Stimulation (PES) require predicting tremor signals in real time to overcome hardware latency. In this study, long short-term memory (LSTM) neural networks were evaluated to predict tremor signals using kinematic data recorded from Essential Tremor (ET) and Parkinson's Disease (PD) patients. The proposed LSTM-based models were capable of predicting both phase and amplitude of tremor signals, outperforming previous methods and providing a robust foundation for PES-based closed-loop tremor suppression."
